Block-Diffusion OD Planning — v4 Results (EOS-fixed backbones)

2026-07-27 · This report is built entirely on the v4 round: all seven mask backbones retrained after the EOS boundary fix, all discriminators and evaluations redone on top of them. The previous consolidated report (RESULTS_BD.pdf) remains the record of the pre-fix (v2) backbones and is referenced here only as the before-side of the fix. Naming: every v4 artifact carries the _v4 tag — checkpoints BD_porto_v3_normal_mask_blk{B}_v4_bd.pth (the v3 token is the dataset index porto v3-0.05, not a model round), discs BDdisc_*_v4.pth, pools *_pool_v4_*, eval tags v4m*/v4f01m*.

1. The EOS boundary fix

The training canvas is [dst, v0, …, vL-1=dst, END…END | PAD…], where END fills only the remainder of the block containing dst and later blocks are PAD (excluded from loss). Under that spec a sample whose dst lands exactly on a block boundary carries zero END supervision — a 1/B fraction of the data, and for B=1 every sample. The v2 blk1 backbone therefore never learned to terminate: unconditionally, 100% of its generations ran to the canvas cap (length 101, 50.5% invalid edges, valid rate 0.000). At B≥2 the same defect appeared as a systematic deficit of boundary-ending lengths: the generated share of lengths with (L+1) mod B = 0 was roughly half the real share (blk2 0.234 vs 0.500; blk32 0.011 vs 0.027).

Fix (bd_models.py build_canvas()): when the END tail would be empty, extend the canvas by one full block of END tokens, included in the loss. Every sample now trains ≥1 END target; for B=1 that is exactly one END token per sample. Verified by smoke_test_bd.py (canvas spec, both kernels' forward/backward and planning).

blk64 is unchanged by construction: no real path in this dataset has L=63 or 127 (boundary share ≈ 0.000), so the fix touches zero blk64 training samples; with identical seeding the v4 blk64 backbone reproduces v2's numbers exactly. It is retained for completeness.

5. Controlled comparison with the published baseline: D-CBG (Schiff et al.)

Plug-in port of Simple Guidance Mechanisms for Discrete Diffusion Models (dcbg_plugin.py, their _cbg_denoise logic verbatim), re-run on the v4 backbones. What differs from IW is method-intrinsic only: D-CBG scores noisy states pφ(y | xt, t) and tilts the per-position categorical, whereas IW scores clean partial paths and reweights joint candidates. Everything else is matched — same adjacency-aware architecture (-adj 1), same 1% data budget and split seeds, same model-generated negatives (each block's own v4 unconditional pool, corrupted by the model's forward process), same 1,000 reserved except_0 pairs, seed 7.

γ = 1 for both methods. Earlier rounds ran D-CBG at γ = 4 while IW ran at its default γ = 1; that compared the two at different guidance strengths. Here both use the exact, untempered form — IW's wn = exp(γ·ℓφ) reduces to the plain density ratio D/(1−D), and D-CBG's log pθ + γ·log pφ reduces to the plain Bayes tilt. (For reference, the v1-era γ sweep on v2 backbones: D-CBG blk4 valid 0.262 / 0.273 / 0.289 at γ = 1 / 2 / 4 — the method needs γ ≫ 1 to move at all.)

Exact vs first-order is a runtime flag on the same classifier (-approx), not a separate model. exact: enumerate all V = 1,393 tokens at the reveal position — under first-hitting only that one position is consumed per step, so this is exact, not an approximation. first-order: their Taylor variant, one classifier forward+backward on the one-hot input per reveal.

5.6 Wall-clock and scorer calls — like-for-like

What the timer measures, exactly: the wall-clock of one planner call on a batch of 100 OD pairs — from handing in the (origin, destination) lists to receiving the finished vertex paths — divided by 100. Inside: denoiser forward passes, guidance scoring (discriminator / classifier), candidate sampling, per-reveal bookkeeping, the planner's own CPU sync and stop scan, path assembly. Outside: checkpoint/data loading, P1/P3 post-processing, EM/PC/DTW scoring, and everything else after the planner returns. So the number is the amortised per-path cost when 100 paths are planned concurrently, not the latency of planning one path on its own (which is far higher per path on an under-utilised GPU).

Fairness controls (time_guidance.py): identical OD pairs, identical batch, identical seed for every method; one method at a time, sequentially, on one GPU that the script verifies is idle — it queries nvidia-smi for resident compute apps and refuses to run if any are present; a warm-up batch per (block, method) that is executed and discarded, so CUDA context creation and kernel autotuning never land in a reported number; every timed region wrapped in torch.cuda.synchronize(); and 3 timed batches per cell that are different slices of the reserved pool (pairs 1–100, 101–200, 201–300), identical across methods. Reported as mean ± std over those 3 batches, so the spread reflects batch composition as well as run-to-run jitter. 300 timed paths per cell, 35 cells.

Not comparable to: (i) the 0.63 s/path printed by the evaluation run for D-CBG-exact at blk4 — that is eval_dcbg.py's sec_per_path, whose timer spans generation plus P1/P3 splicing and EM/PC scoring, with four processes competing for CPU, against 0.328 here for planning alone; (ii) the s/path column of §3, from eval_bd_sweep_controlled.py, which spans generation plus EM/PC, DTW and length scoring over 1,000 paths. 7. Discriminator validity (summary)

Full analysis in DISC_VALIDITY.pdf / DISC_VALIDITY_KO.pdf. Headlines: (i) seen vs unseen discs score held-out inputs near-identically (logit Pearson 0.81–0.98); (ii) on the model-negative task, zero-shot AUC loss is ≤0.05 in both probe directions — most of the signal (edge legality vs the input adjacency, model artifacts) is scenario-agnostic by construction; (iii) the harder data-negative probe (real vs real) separates the two training regimes: the single-scenario e0 disc drops −0.100 (fam 0.05) / −0.084 (fam 0.1) AUC zero-shot, while the 99-scenario e99 disc is nearly flat — multi-scenario training is what buys genuine scenario-specific generalization; (iv) the v2 blk1 "best AUC" was an artifact of its degenerate negative pool and disappears after the EOS fix (0.985 → 0.870), confirming the negative-pool confound analysis.
